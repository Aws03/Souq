# ADR-0028: Shopping: a server-side basket and one pricing pipeline

- **Status:** Accepted (implemented in Phase 8), 2026-09-11.
- **Fixes:** Phase 0 C12, the persistence half: the cart was lost on refresh. The shipping half stays with Phase 12.
- **Closes:** the question Phase 6 left open ([ADR-0026](0026-inventory-reservations.md)): baskets do not reserve stock.
- **Builds on:** [ADR-0004](0004-module-boundaries.md) (the Shopping module), [ADR-0022](0022-tenancy-enforcement.md) (tenancy), [ADR-0023](0023-sessions-and-credentials.md) (sessions) and [ADR-0026](0026-inventory-reservations.md) (only checkout reserves). It changes none of them.
- **Date:** 2026-09-11
- **Related modules:** Shopping; Ordering; Promotions; Inventory; Shipping
- **Related ADRs:** builds on [ADR-0004](0004-module-boundaries.md), [ADR-0022](0022-tenancy-enforcement.md), [ADR-0023](0023-sessions-and-credentials.md) and [ADR-0026](0026-inventory-reservations.md), and deletes baskets on the erasure of [ADR-0027](0027-customer-profile-and-erasure.md); checkout reads the basket itself in [ADR-0029](0029-orders-lifecycle.md); the coupon stage is completed by [ADR-0030](0030-coupon-redemptions.md) and the shipping stage by [ADR-0032](0032-shipping-methods.md); the sign-in merge is repeated for the wishlist in [ADR-0033](0033-review-moderation-and-wishlist.md)

## Context

Before Phase 8 the cart lived only in React memory (`CartContext`):
- A refresh lost it, and it could not follow a customer to another device.
- Its totals were computed in the browser from the price captured when the product was added.
- Checkout priced the same lines again on the server, with its own code.
- The coupon preview was a third path: `GET /api/coupons/apply`, with a subtotal sent by the client.

Money was computed in three places.

## Problem

We need a basket that:
- persists for guests and customers, and merges the two at sign-in;
- keeps prices live;
- shows exactly what checkout will charge.

And it must do that without these risks:
- reserving stock for carts;
- trusting the client for any amount;
- letting the anonymous basket become a way to read someone else's basket or to guess coupon codes.

## Options considered

| Question | Chosen | Rejected (why) |
|---|---|---|
| Where the basket lives | **On the server:** a `Basket` aggregate with `BasketLine`s (variant plus quantity). One basket per owner per store | Browser storage only: lost across devices, and its totals can't be trusted. A server basket with totals computed by the client |
| Guest identity | A random 256-bit token in an HttpOnly, Secure, `SameSite=Strict` cookie scoped to `/api/basket`. Only its SHA-256 is stored | An id generated by the client and kept in `localStorage`: readable by injected scripts, and guessable if weak. A server session for every visitor: state for every crawler |
| Prices in the basket | Never stored. Every read prices the lines from the live catalog | A snapshot at add time: a stale price is shown, then checkout charges a different total |
| Computing totals | **One pipeline, `IPricing`** (a Shopping contract): subtotal → discount → shipping → tax → total. The basket view and `CreateOrderHandler` both use it | Separate calculations for the basket and checkout: they drift, and the phase's exit criterion forbids it |
| A coupon that can't be used | Reported inside the quote (`applied: false`, with the code and message). Checkout turns it into the same 422 codes as before | A 422 for the whole basket read: the basket disappears because a code is wrong |
| Coupon guessing | A coupon is priced only through `GET /api/basket/quote`, behind the existing coupon-preview rate limit | A coupon on every basket read: unlimited guessing |
| Stock | **Baskets reserve nothing.** Adding or increasing beyond what is available now gets `422 InsufficientStock`. A line whose stock later fell below its quantity is flagged and blocks checkout | Reserving on add: abandoned carts lock stock, and a bot can empty a store |
| Sign-in | A customer's first basket request that still carries a guest cookie merges the guest basket into theirs, deletes it and clears the cookie. Quantities are summed and capped at 99 per line; lines beyond 50 are dropped | Merging in the login endpoint: the cookie is scoped to `/api/basket` and never reaches `/api/auth`. Keeping two baskets |
| Concurrency | The last write wins (no `rowversion`). Unique owner indexes stop duplicate baskets, and checkout re-validates everything | Optimistic concurrency: a 409 on a cart edit is noise for a record that isn't financial |
| Expiry | Sliding, from the last change: 30 days for guests and 180 for customers (`Basket:*`). A per-store hosted sweep deletes expired baskets, up to 500 per run. Reading an expired guest basket deletes it | No expiry: anonymous visitors make the table grow without bound |
| Staff accounts | They have no purchase profile, so they get a guest basket, like any visitor | A 403 on the basket: the storefront would break for staff who browse it |

## Decision

The options marked "Chosen" above. Details:

- **Shipping and tax are explicit zero stages.**
  - Shipping is charged from Phase 12.
  - There is no tax model and no planned phase for one, so it is recorded as an open product decision (**P-06**).
  - The totals shown are what orders charge today.
  - The stages already have their place in the pipeline and in the contract, so adding them does not change the API.
- **Checkout** still receives its lines from the client in Phase 8; Phase 9 converts the basket itself. It prices them with the same pipeline, so the basket total and the order total are equal by construction. An integration test places an order with the basket's lines and coupon, then compares subtotal, discount and total.
- **Module map:**
  - `Features/Baskets` is the Shopping module.
  - Ordering may call `Baskets.Contracts` (`IPricing`).
  - Shopping may call `Inventory.Contracts` (`IStockAvailability`).
  - The architecture test allows exactly these references, with no cycles.
  - The folder name is plural because a `Basket` namespace would shadow the `Basket` entity.
- **Endpoints:** everything is under `/api/basket` and anonymous by design. Every request acts only on the caller's own basket: the customer's, or the one the guest token opens in the host's store.
- **Rate limits:** writes allow 120 per minute per host and client address; quotes share the coupon-preview limit of 30 per minute.
- **Erasure:** erasing a customer ([ADR-0027](0027-customer-profile-and-erasure.md)) deletes their basket.
- **Deferred:** category visibility at checkout and the sellable-items contract remain in Phase 9, as they are for checkout today.

## Consequences

- **Positive:**
  - The basket survives a refresh and a device switch (C12).
  - Money is computed in one place.
  - Carts lock no stock.
  - A leaked database does not expose guest tokens (only hashes are stored), and scripts can't read them (HttpOnly).
- **Negative / limits:**
  - Two simultaneous first adds from a brand-new guest can create two baskets. The later cookie wins, and the other basket expires unused.
  - When two tabs edit the same line, the last write wins.
  - A line that became unavailable stays visible, flagged, until the customer removes it. This is by design: nothing disappears silently.
  - Shipping and tax stay at zero until Phase 12 and the P-06 decision.
  - Checking out from the basket itself, and clearing the basket on the server when an order is placed, are Phase 9. Today the frontend clears the basket after payment.
